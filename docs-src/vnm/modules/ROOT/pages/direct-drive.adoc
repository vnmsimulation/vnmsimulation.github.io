= *Introduction to VNM Direct Drive*

The *VNM Direct Drive Wheelbase* family is a professional-grade sim racing ecosystem designed for high-precision driving simulation. These wheelbases utilize a *5-polepair smooth motor* and a high-resolution *23-bit encoder* to deliver detailed Force Feedback (FFB) that reproduces road surfaces, tire grip, and vehicle dynamics. The product line is designed for versatility and power, offering four distinct models to suit various levels of sim racing needs:

* *Premier*: 13Nm maximum torque.
* *Elite*: 18Nm maximum torque.
* *Supreme*: 25Nm maximum torque.
* *Xtreme*: 32Nm maximum torque.

The system is managed through the *VNM SimCenter* software, which provides a unified platform for device detection, pedal calibration, firmware updates, and advanced FFB tuning.
