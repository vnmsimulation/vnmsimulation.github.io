Introduction to VNM Direct Drive

The VNM Direct Drive Wheelbase family is a professional-grade sim racing ecosystem designed for high-precision driving simulation. These wheelbases utilize a 5-polepair smooth motor and a high-resolution 23-bit encoder to deliver detailed Force Feedback (FFB) that reproduces road surfaces, tire grip, and vehicle dynamics. The product line is designed for versatility and power, offering four distinct models to suit various levels of sim racing needs:

  • Premier: 13Nm maximum torque.

  • Elite: 18Nm maximum torque.

  • Supreme: 25Nm maximum torque.

  • Xtreme: 32Nm maximum torque.

The system is managed through the VNM SimCenter software, which provides a unified platform for device detection, pedal calibration, firmware updates, and advanced FFB tuning.

Overview of Related Documentation

The provided documents serve as a comprehensive guide for the physical setup and digital optimization of the VNM ecosystem:

  • VNM Sim Center Configuration Manual (v0.6): This document focuses on the software side of the experience. It details the installation of VNM SimCenter, system requirements (Windows 10/11 64-bit), and the configuration of device parameters. It includes in-depth explanations of Basic and Advanced wheelbase settings, such as torque modes, filtering, and "User Effects" like damping and friction. Furthermore, it covers the Telemetry system, which allows users to generate additional haptic feedback for gear shifts, ABS activity, and wheel spin based on real-time game data.

  • VNM Direct Drive User Manual (Revision 1.0): This manual provides the technical specifications and physical installation procedures for the hardware. It outlines the mounting patterns (front, side, and bottom) for different models and details the necessary connection ports. A significant portion of this document is dedicated to safety protocols, emphasizing the risks associated with high-torque motors, proper power-on sequences, and the essential use of the Emergency Stop (EMC) button.
